HOD Profile

Prof. Sreeja.K

Head of Department — MCA

Academic & Professional Profile

Prof. Sreeja.K has obtained her Masters (MCA) from Govt. Engineering College, Thrissur, Kerala. She joined Mohandas College of Engineering in 2003 as a System Analyst. She was in charge of monitoring administration and analysis of the campus automation software, design and administration of the campus network, as well as data integration spread across the institution.


She has been working as an Assistant Professor in the Department of MCA since 2007. From 2014 onwards, she has also been handling the responsibility of Head of Department of MCA.

Previous Experience

Before joining MCET, she worked as a System Analyst at the State Project Director's Office, DPEP, Trivandrum, as Head of the State MIS Unit of the project implementation body.


She was involved in educational planning, budget preparation, monitoring and analysis of key variables and data in project achievement across various fields, and coordinating these inputs towards overall project achievement.


She was also a member of the National Implementation Team of DISE (District Information System for Education).
